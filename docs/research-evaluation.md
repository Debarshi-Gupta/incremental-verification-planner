# Research and evaluation

Selective verification is an optimization backed by evidence, not proof that omitted tests would pass. The planner uses deterministic graph traversal, policy risk contributions, weighted impact coverage, confidence, and marginal coverage per conservative runtime cost. Audits compare outcomes only for equivalent source, dependency, configuration, toolchain, environment, and required-service fingerprints.

Historical replay reports comparable sample counts, runtime and compute reduction, observed regression recall, fallback rates, audit miss rate with Wilson confidence intervals, and incomparable runs. Calibration and evaluation periods are split chronologically. Risk-based CI gating remains off until representative held-out repositories and change classes meet organization-approved thresholds.
